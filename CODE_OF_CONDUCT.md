# Code of Conduct

Role-Aware Dashboard Toolkit expects respectful, professional collaboration in
all contribution channels. This policy applies to everyone, including
maintainers, in project issues, pull requests, discussions, and other
project-related communication.

## Expected behavior

- Be constructive and specific in technical feedback.
- Focus criticism on code and decisions, not people.
- Respect maintainer review boundaries and project scope.
- Protect confidential information and respect others' privacy.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, personal attacks, or discriminatory language.
- Repeated hostile or disruptive communication.
- Sharing sensitive or private material without permission.
- Retaliation against someone for reporting a concern in good faith.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers review reports and relevant context, then take proportionate
action. Responses may include a request to change behavior, a warning, content
removal, or temporary or permanent restrictions on project participation.
Serious violations may result in immediate restrictions.

Reports are handled discreetly, with information shared only as needed to review
and respond to the concern. When another uninvolved maintainer is available,
they should handle reports involving a maintainer.

## Reporting

Use the [issue tracker](https://github.com/Mike-Byrne-AI/role-aware-dashboard-toolkit/issues)
to open an issue asking a maintainer to arrange a private
contact channel. Include only the contact request: do not post incident
details, personal information, or names of the people involved. Wait for a
maintainer to arrange a private channel before sharing details.

See [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for vulnerability reporting.
